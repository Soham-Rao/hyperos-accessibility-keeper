The app uses device-provided system fonts and some Android framework drawable references. Installed app names/icons are loaded from the device, not bundled as our own artwork. Project vector icons and the README sample-data SVG illustrations were created for this fork. No external font, stock image, icon pack or screenshot was added to the APK by this work.
